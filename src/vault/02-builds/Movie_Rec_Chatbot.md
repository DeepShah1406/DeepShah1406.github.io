# Movie Recommendation Chatbot

> [!ACHIEVEMENT]
> GitHub: [DeepShah1406/Movies-Series-Recomendation-Chatbot](https://github.com/DeepShah1406/Movies-Series-Recomendation-Chatbot)

## Overview
A conversational AI engine that helps users discover movies and series across Hollywood, Bollywood, and international cinema.

## Technical Highlights
- **LLM Reasoning**: Uses **Llama-3.3-70b** to understand natural language preferences (e.g., "Find me a sci-fi thriller with a twist ending").
- **API Integration**: Dynamically fetches metadata and high-quality posters from **OMDb** and **TMDb** APIs.
- **Multilingual Search**: Specialized support for regional Indian cinema (Tollywood, Kollywood) as well as K-Dramas.

## Tech Stack
- **AI**: Groq API.
- **Data**: OMDb & TMDb APIs.
- **UI**: Streamlit.
