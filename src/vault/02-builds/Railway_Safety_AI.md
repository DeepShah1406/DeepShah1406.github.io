# Railway Safety AI (Computer Vision)

> [!ACHIEVEMENT]
> Focus: Real-time hazard detection and human tracking.

## Context
During my tenure as a Stereo-Vision & CV Intern, I worked on advanced tracking systems designed for high-stakes environments where depth and distance are critical for safety.

## Technical Breakdown
- **Stereo Calibration**: Built and calibrated dual-camera systems to accurately calculate depth and distance of human subjects.
- **3D Pose Estimation**: Utilized **MediaPipe** and **MMPose** to map human joints in 3D space, allowing for precise movement analysis.
- **YOLO Integration**: Deployed YOLO models for rapid object detection and segmentation.

## Safety Impact
- Optimized depth estimation accuracy for improved tracking in variable lighting.
- Reduced hazard detection latency, potentially preventing industrial accidents.
