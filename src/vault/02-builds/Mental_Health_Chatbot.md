# Mental Health Chatbot (RAG)

> [!ACHIEVEMENT]
> GitHub: [DeepShah1406/Mental-Health-Chatbot](https://github.com/DeepShah1406/Mental-Health-Chatbot)

## Overview
A specialized chatbot designed to provide real-time, reliable mental health support. It uses RAG to ground its advice in authoritative medical documentation while maintaining a compassionate tone.

## Tech Stack
- **Core**: Python 3.13, LangChain.
- **LLM**: Groq (Llama-3.3-70b-versatile).
- **Vector DB**: ChromaDB with HuggingFace Embeddings.
- **Frontend**: Streamlit.

## Key Features
- **Authoritative Sourcing**: Fetches data exclusively from **WHO** and **MoHFW** (India) documents.
- **Safety Guardrails**: Automated detection of "self-harm" or distress keywords to trigger immediate emergency resource delivery.
- **Contextual Awareness**: Maintains empathy while providing scientifically accurate information.
